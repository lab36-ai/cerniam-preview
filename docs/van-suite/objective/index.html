01 / The business opportunity

The opportunity is already inside the business.

Mid-market companies generate signals about demand, customer intent, available capacity, supplier alternatives, product performance, margin, and emerging markets every day.

Those signals are scattered across sales systems, inventory platforms, supplier portals, financial reports, customer service tools, spreadsheets, and the experience carried by individual employees. Opportunity disappears when the business cannot connect them in time.

When those systems are integrated, the same operating picture can reveal where to grow revenue, improve customer value, deploy capacity, strengthen margin, and prevent leakage.

Cerniam helps leaders see the upside and downside together, then act on the best available opportunity.

Project Woven

Operational AI for opportunities and risks while action can still change the outcome.

Project Woven combines live enterprise events, Operational Knowledge Graphs, specialized AI agents, deterministic controls, and human authority. The system maintains a continuously updated understanding of customers, products, orders, inventory, suppliers, locations, assets, workflows, commitments, and risk.

Behind that operating experience are event streaming, semantic retrieval, graph reasoning, machine learning, model adaptation, distributed agents, and real-time data infrastructure. Cerniam absorbs that complexity so the customer does not have to.
