Research question

Can a ranked prediction remain useful after the outcome becomes observable?

This walkthrough separates observed history, candidate ranking, and actual outcome so that a prediction can be inspected rather than merely announced. Scores and outcomes are representative until connected to a verified model artifact.

Method

What the instrument calculates

Scenario selection updates the observed sequence, actual next basket, top-10 candidate ranking, hit highlighting, Hit Count, Recall@10, and first-hit rank.

Production boundary

What must change before deployment

Match stable product IDs rather than labels; display decision scores after compatibility, availability, and eligibility filters; preserve model scores for diagnostics and model evaluation.

Example contract

Replaceable data source

The renderer is framework-neutral and can be connected to a ranking service or replayed Progressor events without changing the visual evidence model.
