Research question

What changes when a transformer is connected to operational memory?

A transformer can rank what may happen next. Operational memory gives those candidates a living world: product relationships, basket state, availability, eligibility, policy, timing, and outcome history. Cerniam uses that context to determine what the enterprise should do now.

Architecture narrative / Closed operational loop

A custom transformer connected to operational memory.

The model is not the operating system. It is a predictive organ inside a wider decision architecture.
