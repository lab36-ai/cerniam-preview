Research instrument / Architecture atlas

From source systems to inspectable intelligence.

Source systems of record remain authoritative for their own domains. Cerniam Cortex maps their contracts and signals into source-stamped context, while the Intelligence Plane compares deterministic and agentic reasoning.

The research question is not simply whether a system can recommend an action. It is whether that action can be traced, explained, replayed, and verified against the state that produced it.

01Authority stays put.

Operational traffic remains on its native path and each source retains domain authority.

02Context carries provenance.

Every projected fact remains source-stamped, time-aware, and inspectable.

03Intelligence leaves evidence.

Recommendations and governed actions produce traces, receipts, and replayable state.

Research boundary

This instrument describes an inspectability architecture. It does not imply that Cortex replaces systems of record or assumes their execution authority.
