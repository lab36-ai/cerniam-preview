Van Oleson / Founder and present steward

A single focus for thirty years.

Van Oleson’s career has centered on one consequential question: how can an enterprise understand what is happening now, distinguish what matters, protect what must continue, and act before the opportunity or recovery window closes?

01 / The question beneath the systems

The technologies changed.
The operating question did not.

Across airlines, logistics, network computing, commerce, retail, and enterprise platforms, the work has returned to the same problem: turn live operational activity into trusted understanding, then route that understanding toward the right decision.

Events made operations visible. Operational state made them understandable. Context made them connected. Machine learning made patterns more discoverable. Governed agents make intelligence actionable inside explicit authority boundaries.

03 / Return to applied research

From leading enterprise platforms back to the research question.

After leading technology at L Brands and Victoria’s Secret, and after deploying AI applications across retail operations and customer experience, Oleson stepped away from the CTO role to return to applied research.

The objective was not to place an assistant above disconnected enterprise software. It was to design the operating architecture beneath intelligent action: live events, semantic context, machine reasoning, explicit policy, human authority, and verifiable outcomes working as one system.

04 / The modern expression

Cerniam carries the work forward.

Cerniam partners with mid-market companies to integrate their systems and simplify sophisticated AI technologies. The business purpose is direct: discover opportunities, maximize revenue, stop avoidable loss, and place enterprise-class decision capabilities within reach.
