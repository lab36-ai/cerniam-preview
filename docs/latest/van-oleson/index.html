03 / A pioneering team inside the enterprise

We helped pioneer mission-vital, enterprise-scale event systems.

At Delta Air Lines, our enterprise engineering team and Georgia Tech systems researchers innovated event methods from inside one of the world’s most demanding operating environments. But the event was not the only point. The deeper invention was the real-time operational model around it.

An event told us something changed. The operational model told us what it meant, what else was affected, and what the enterprise could do next.

05 / Context, memory, and the event that matters

The event was not the only point.

Context, memory, and the real-time operational model gave the event meaning.

In airline operations, an aircraft event does not stand alone. Its meaning depends on the flight, airport, crew, passengers, baggage, maintenance, weather, gates, downstream connections, service commitments, and every other state that may be affected.

Since 1997, my research focus has been to react to state change, place it inside a living operational context, and derive the event that matters. Modern Operational AI extends that same idea by giving the changing world around the event more memory, meaning, and reasoning power.
