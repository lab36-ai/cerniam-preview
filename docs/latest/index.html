Case study  /  Order fulfillment

The website took the order.
The warehouse can’t ship it.

A customer orders six units for delivery Friday. The website shows them in stock.

But the inventory is spread across locations. The assigned warehouse has only four available. Two more are sitting at another facility — and the systems don’t put that whole picture together.

See the problem.

Cerniam connects the order, inventory, warehouse and fulfillment data.

It sees immediately that this warehouse can’t fulfill the order as promised.

Find the best option.

Ship from two locations? Move inventory? Fulfill from another warehouse?

Cerniam compares the options — including cost, speed and customer impact — and identifies the best one.

Fix it in time.

The order can be rerouted before it becomes a late shipment, a cancellation or an angry customer.

Not three days later when the customer calls.While there’s still time to fix it.
