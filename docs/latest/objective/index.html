01 / The business reality

The systems know.
The business still waits.

Mid-market leaders are asked to make consequential decisions using information scattered across sales systems, inventory platforms, supplier portals, financial reports, customer service tools, spreadsheets, and the experience carried by individual employees.

Each system may hold part of the truth. Few provide a complete picture of what is happening across the business at this moment. The result is familiar: teams reconcile data manually, decisions arrive late, and the organization responds after a risk has already become a cost.

Cerniam is designed to bring that operational picture together and help leaders act on it.

02 / The decisions we mean

Business pressure is connected.
Decisions should be too.

These are examples of the operating questions advanced decision intelligence can help a company resolve.

Retail

Protect the sale before inventory becomes the constraint.

Recognize how demand, supplier delays, available stock, customer commitments, and fulfillment cost interact. Evaluate where inventory should move before a shortage becomes a lost sale.

Distribution

Recover a commitment before the customer feels the delay.

Connect supplier risk to affected orders, available alternatives, transportation options, margin, and working capital. Show which response best protects the customer and the business.

Manufacturing

See the downstream consequence of an operational change.

Understand how equipment, material, labor, quality, and schedule constraints affect production and delivery. Compare recovery options before separate issues become a systemic disruption.

Travel and transportation

Choose the recovery that protects the whole operation.

Evaluate weather, equipment, staffing, customer connections, and downstream commitments together. Prioritize the response that preserves critical operations and reduces total recovery cost.

Project Woven

Operational AI for decisions while action can still change the outcome.

Project Woven combines live enterprise events, Operational Knowledge Graphs, specialized AI agents, deterministic controls, and human authority. The system maintains a continuously updated understanding of customers, products, orders, inventory, suppliers, locations, assets, workflows, commitments, and risk.

Behind that operating experience are event streaming, semantic retrieval, graph reasoning, machine learning, model adaptation, distributed agents, and real-time data infrastructure. Cerniam absorbs that complexity so the customer does not have to.
