Services

Solving complex enterprise problems with intelligence that works in the real world.

Cerniam partners with organizations to design and build agentic systems that understand context, reason across your environment, and help your team act with clarity and speed.

1. Our Approach: From Problem to Capability

We deliver working systems—and we leave you with the capability to run them.

  1. 1

    Assess

    We understand your operating problem, existing systems, data, contracts, constraints, and desired outcomes. The result is clarity on what matters most.

  2. 2

    Design

    We develop a proposed architecture and implementation plan—including the agents, context, workflows, integrations, and measures of success—for your approval.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build and integrate the agreed solution: agents, knowledge models, workflows, mappings, and the supporting architecture needed to operate in your environment.

  4. 4

    Transfer

    We train your team to operate, manage, and extend the solution with confidence. You own it. We don’t run it for you.

We build the capability. Your team owns it.

2. Areas of Expertise

We bring deep experience across the technical and operational challenges required to put AI agents to work in real enterprises.

Agentic Harness Design

Design the controls, context, tools, permissions, evaluation, and operational infrastructure required to put agents to work safely inside real enterprise environments.

Enterprise Knowledge & Context Architecture

Design operational knowledge graphs and context models that give AI systems an understanding of entities, relationships, state, and dependencies across the enterprise.

Agentic Workflow & Decision Systems

Identify business processes where agents can interpret changing conditions, reason across enterprise context, and coordinate or recommend actions.

Enterprise Integration & Contract Mapping

Assess existing systems and interfaces, discover contracts, and design mappings that allow new capabilities and agents to work with the environment already in place.

Operational AI Architecture

Design the architecture required to move AI from experiments into mission-critical operations—including reliability, observability, resilience, governance, and human oversight.

Legacy Modernization & Modular Architecture

Help enterprises introduce new capabilities around existing systems without a wholesale rip-and-replace—identifying what should stay, what should change, and where an intelligent layer can reduce integration complexity.

High-Scale, Mission-Critical Systems

Architecture and technical advisory for systems where availability, real-time response, resilience, and scale aren’t optional.
