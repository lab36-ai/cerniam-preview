Integration shouldn’t be the project.

Traditional integrations require teams to understand interfaces on both sides, define how data and business objects correspond, build mappings, and maintain that custom integration as systems change.

Cerniam Cortex changes that.

Cortex discovers the contracts exposed by your existing systems and generates the mappings between those systems and Cerniam’s pre-integrated enterprise-grade capabilities.

Instead of asking the enterprise to adapt itself to new software, Cerniam adapts the connection to the enterprise.
